# Adoption v2.6 — activity and affordability

Accepted 21 October 2026. Supersedes v2.5 zero-weight token prices.

Five pillars remain 20% each. Adoption now assigns 5% of the whole composite
to each of Ramp token volume, OpenRouter token volume, Bloomberg SDLLMTK
affordability and Ramp effective-token affordability. Higher volume and lower
unit costs raise their respective scores. Other pillars and their transformations
are unchanged. Survey breadth stays at zero weight.

## Price scoring

Signal = −ln(positive USD per million tokens).
Score = clip(50 + 15 × (signal − baseline mean) / baseline sample standard
deviation, 0, 100). Each provider has its own mean and standard deviation.
Volume retains its existing log-volume treatment and calibration.

Bloomberg uses complete saved calendar-month means. PREV-filled dates are not
independently verified fresh quotes. Ramp uses matched-model spending divided
by matched-model tokens, multiplied by one million, across four completed
Monday–Sunday weeks ending on the final Sunday in the month. All four weeks
must have at least 95% matched token coverage. The monthly price is the ratio
of totals, not the arithmetic mean of weekly prices.

Both price signals use exactly seven accepted calibration months, December
2025–June 2026. This is a provisional, retrospective baseline. All seven must
remain available or builds fail, rather than silently shrink calibration.
Post-June observations do not enter calibration. Source revisions within the
accepted history may change estimated parameters, as with existing builders.

There is no synthesized earlier price history or redistribution of missing
weights. The v2.6 composite is withheld before December 2025 and whenever any
component is unavailable. Prices can reflect mix, caching and commercial terms;
these are affordability proxies, not constant-quality repricing indices.

## Spending diagnostic

The page separately displays matched Ramp nominal spending, changes against
the preceding monthly window, and changes against the same month a year earlier.
Spending = effective price × matched tokens / one million, using identical
model/week pairs. It has zero additional score weight. Adjacent four-week
windows leave a one-week gap when month-end Sundays are five weeks apart.
Changing matched-model composition can affect comparisons. These are observation
windows, not calendar-month recognized revenue or industry revenue estimates.
Bloomberg prices are never multiplied by unrelated OpenRouter traffic.

## Comparison and operation

On the same refreshed source vintage, June changes from 69.1504793842 in v2.5
to 67.1907402361 in v2.6, a methodology effect of −1.9597391482 points.
Earlier recorded v2.5 values used a different Ramp vintage. Conditional CapEx
projections are rebuilt from the revised June anchor; other assumptions remain.

Each month retains previousVersionScore. The accepted pre-change snapshot is
archived at data/reference/core-v2.5.ts; it is not a regular builder input.
The comparison audit is data/derived/audits/adoption-v2.6.json.

The core/all dependency chains run build-affordability.py after the reproducible
v2.5 intermediate and before projection. The refresh runner automatically uses
v2.6. The manifest hashes both the builder and affordability helper. Price
snapshots now contain monthlyScoring and price weight 0.10; token snapshots
report aggregate volume weight 0.10. Sync, rebuild and restart the local website
after building data, or use the development server for reload.
