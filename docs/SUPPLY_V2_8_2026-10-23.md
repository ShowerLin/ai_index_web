# Supply v2.8 — first-stage implementation

Implemented 23 October 2026. Supply replaces the Hyperscaler CapEx pillar and retains 20% of the composite. No electrical-equipment shipment, order or lead-time series is added to Supply. The pre-existing power-equipment orders input in the separate Investment pillar remains unchanged.

## Historical score

Composite weights: five-company historical CapEx growth 10%, DDR5 spot-price momentum 5%, H100 rental-price momentum 5%. These are provisional implementation weights. Other four pillars are unchanged from v2.7. Higher Supply means stronger investment and hardware pricing pressure; it does not mean more physical capacity or less scarcity.

CapEx keeps its existing trailing-four-quarter YoY growth and baseline. Price momentum is monthly source-level YoY growth, normalized as clip(50 + 15 × (growth − mean) / sample standard deviation, 0, 100). Both price signals use the six January–June 2026 observations for fixed, retrospective, provisional calibration. Later observations do not change that calibration. Source revisions within the baseline can revise it. Partial months are excluded, and missing scores withhold the composite without redistributing weights.

June composite: 62.2652089766 under v2.7 → 62.2013213936 under v2.8, a methodology effect of −0.0638875830 points on the same saved observations. June Supply score: 63.7557455993. Latest complete composite remains June 2026. July–September remain incomplete. The old snapshot is preserved in data/reference/core-v2.7.ts.
