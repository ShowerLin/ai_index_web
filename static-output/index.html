SCORING LOGIC

Resilience first; spending momentum second.

Resilience combines revenue growth (20%), EBITDA margin (25%), FCF / EBITDA (25%), inverse net debt / EBITDA (20%) and interest coverage (10%). Missing interest coverage is excluded and the remaining weights are renormalized.

Date treatment

Companies without an interim report remain on 2026-03-31. They receive no freshness penalty; the reporting cohort stays visible.

Use case

Cross-sectional quality screening only. No valuation, consensus, price target or position-sizing conclusion is implied.

07 · METHODOLOGY & SOURCE CONTROL

Transparent by design.

The five-pillar core index remains the official momentum measure. A separately labeled experimental overlay translates financial quality and CDS conditions into bounded adjustments; it does not rewrite the core history.

01

Normalize momentum

Each included series is converted to YoY change, then standardized against its own available history. This avoids adding incomparable source-native units.

02

Score each pillar

Signal score = 50 + 15 × z-score, capped at 0–100. Series are equally weighted within each pillar.

03

Build the atmosphere

Adoption, Demand, Investment, Imports and Hyperscaler CapEx receive equal 20% pillar weights. A score above 50 indicates above-history momentum.

04

Add satellites separately

The adjusted score adds bounded fundamental and credit deltas to the core. Bond issuance stays at zero weight until the missing comparison month and historical normalization are resolved. LLM token pricing is context-only until it has a defensible YoY history.

EXPERIMENTAL OVERLAY FORMULA

Adjusted = Core + Fundamental Δ + Credit Δ

Fundamental Δ = 10% × (median Fundamental Composite − 50)

Credit score = clamp(50 + 2.5 × CDS Change Signal, 0, 100)

Credit Δ = 10% × (Credit score − 50)
